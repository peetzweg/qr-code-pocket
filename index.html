QR Code Pocket

Every QR code you collect, kept in one place. A QR code wallet (or keeper, or holder) for your pocket: Train tickets, museum and attraction entries, restaurant bookings, takeaway orders, boarding passes. Add the screenshot or the PDF from your email; the code is read on your phone and kept ready, full screen, for the gate.

Whether you call it a QR code wallet, a QR code keeper, a holder or a saver: it keeps the codes you'd otherwise hunt for in screenshots and emails, and shows the right one at the gate.

How it works

  • Add a screenshot, a PDF ticket, paste an image, or scan a code with the camera.
  • The QR code, Aztec code or barcode is decoded on your phone, and the date, time, train, seat and booking number are read from the ticket and the code itself.
  • Your tickets sit in a stack like a wallet; tap one and the code opens full screen, large and bright.
  • Mark a ticket as used and it's postmarked into Collected: a record of the trip.
  • Install it to your Home Screen on iPhone or Android. It works offline.

Questions

How do I save a QR code from a screenshot?
Open QR Code Pocket, tap Add, and pick the screenshot from Photos or Files. The code is found and decoded on your phone; the text around it (date, time, train, seat) is read too, so you mostly just confirm.
Can I keep PDF tickets from email?
Yes. Add the PDF from Files: every page is scanned for QR codes, Aztec codes and barcodes, and two tickets for the same booking can become one pass with both codes.
Does it work offline, on a train or a plane?
Yes. Once it's on your Home Screen, the app, the code reader and the text recognition all work without a connection.
Is anything uploaded?
No. Your codes, screenshots and notes stay in your browser's storage on your phone. There's no account.
Will the gate scanner read the code?
The code is redrawn from its exact content, so it reads like the original. If a scanner is fussy, Plain shows a classic black-on-white code, and Original shows your screenshot.
Which codes are supported?
QR codes, Aztec (boarding passes), PDF417, Data Matrix and common barcodes such as Code 128, EAN-13 and ITF.
Is it free?
Yes: free, no ads, no tracking.
