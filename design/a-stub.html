A · Stub (硬券)

Paper ticket stubs, after old JR card tickets and museum stubs. Off-white paper and black ink, one faint tint per ticket. Each ticket's 地紋 ground pattern is drawn from its QR bytes, one wavy row per byte, with a guilloche seal, a hex microtext tear line and a serial number. The list is a timeline of slim stubs with the code on the tear-off end.
